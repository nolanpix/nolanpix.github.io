复杂 ECO 工作的组织方式：只设三道关卡Structuring complex ECO work around three gates

大型 ECO 和重度依赖脚本的工作，崩溃的方式往往可以预料：所有东西都在改所有东西，没人说得清新出现的违例是哪次改动引起的；评审要么时时刻刻都在做（进度慢得像在爬），要么根本不做（最后交出一团乱麻）。下面是对我有效的组织方式。

一个执行者，多个只读协作者

负责修复的 driver（执行者）只有一个：改动只落在一个地方，只有一个人或一个进程拥有修改权。围绕它运行的是 read-only workers（只读协作者）：并行的搜索和差异审计，只看不改。它们负责回答问题（“这条线网还出现在哪里？”“这两次运行之间改了什么？”），但不往改动堆里添加自己的修改。

关键是把改变状态的一方和只观察状态的各方分开。出问题时，你只有一个嫌疑对象，而不是十二个。

重量级评审只放在三道关卡

评审很贵，所以要花在回报最高的地方。三道关卡：

  • Plan lock（方案锁定）：动手修改之前，先就方案达成一致。过了这一点，方案就是契约；偏离方案要记录下来，而不是临场发挥。
  • Second repeated error（同类错误第二次出现）：第一次失败可以当作噪声。同一类错误第二次出现时，就该停下来评审——这说明错的是方法，而不是某个笔误。
  • Final signoff（最终签核）：最后对照锁定的方案，完整、仔细地过一遍，只做一次。

关卡之间，放手让执行者跑。每个中间步骤都评审，只会把两小时的 ECO 拖成两天，却抓不到更多 bug。

为什么它站得住

这其实就是后端流程本身赖以运转的思路：改动只有唯一的真实来源，围绕它做低成本的并行观察，昂贵的检查集中放在几个精心挑选的检查点，而不是摊到每一步。

Big ECO and script-heavy work falls apart in a predictable way: everything edits everything, nobody is sure which change caused the new violation, and review either happens constantly (and slows to a crawl) or never (and ships a mess). Here is the shape that has worked for me.

One driver, many read-only workers

There is exactly one driver making fixes - one place where edits land, one person or process that owns the mutation. Around it run read-only workers: parallel search and diff audits that look but never touch. They answer questions ("where else does this net appear?", "what changed between these two runs?") without adding their own edits to the pile.

The point is to separate the thing that changes state from the things that only observe it. When something breaks, you have one suspect, not twelve.

Heavy review at three gates only

Review is expensive, so spend it where it pays off. Three gates:

  • Plan lock - before any edit, agree on the plan. After this point the plan is the contract; deviations get written down, not improvised.
  • Second repeated error - the first failure is noise. The second time the same class of error shows up, stop and review - it means the approach, not the typo, is wrong.
  • Final signoff - the full, careful pass, once, at the end, against the locked plan.

Between the gates, let the driver run. Reviewing every intermediate step is how you turn a two-hour ECO into a two-day one without catching more bugs.

Why it holds up

This is just the same idea the back-end flow already runs on: a single source of truth for the mutation, cheap parallel observation around it, and expensive checks batched at a few well-chosen checkpoints instead of smeared across everything.
