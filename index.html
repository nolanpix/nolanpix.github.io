About & how to use this site

I design the physical back end of digital chips — turning a synthesized netlist into a placed, clocked, routed, timing-closed layout. I'm also always reading around the edges: RTL, synthesis, DFT, verification, and packaging.

How to use it: skim Latest notes for a specific technique, open a repo if you want running code, and ignore the rest. Posts are short and practical — one idea, what I tried, what actually worked. If a note saves you an afternoon, that's the whole point.
